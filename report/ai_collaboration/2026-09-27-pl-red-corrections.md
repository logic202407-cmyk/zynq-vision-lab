# PL red-camera trial corrections

- Screen-derived color thresholds selected only 20 pixels on a retained raw camera frame with the red paper visibly present. The display conversion and auto-exposure made the screen proxy unreliable. The predicate was recalibrated against two private RGB565 frames at different exposures, then applied identically to RTL and software and compared in xsim. Two frames do not establish general lighting robustness.
- The first 100-pair board comparison matched all pairs it could associate, but its receive loop also ran the pixel-by-pixel Python reference. That blocked UDP reception and produced 18 incomplete frames and 12 sequence gaps. The comparator was corrected to collect a bounded number of complete frames in memory first, stop reception, then compare. The repeat had 100 matching pairs, 0 sequence gaps and 0 incomplete frames. The initial losses were a host test-harness artifact, not evidence of a PL frame-rate drop.
- A one-cycle `result_strobe` in the camera-stream adapter test was initially checked after three idle cycles, so the test missed the pulse. The testbench was corrected to latch the strobe when asserted and then check the held result. The RTL was unchanged for that correction.
